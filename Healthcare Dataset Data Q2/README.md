# A. What business problem or opportunity are you trying to understand?
Primary Business Problem & Opportunity:
Healthcare facilities and hospital networks struggle with unpredictable financial margins, inefficient resource allocation (length of stay), and inconsistent quality of care outcomes across patient admissions.

Length of Stay (LOS) Analysis: Calculating the duration between Date of Admission and Discharge Date allows facilities to analyze bed occupancy rates, turn-around times, and bottlenecks by Admission Type (Emergency vs. Elective) or Medical Condition.   

Billing & Pricing Variance: Analyzing Billing Amount across different Insurance Provider options, medical conditions, and admission types to identify pricing inconsistencies, excessive charging, or revenue leakage.   

Diagnostic Accuracy & Treatment Tracking: Evaluating the prevalence of Abnormal or Inconclusive test results across specific hospitals, doctors, or condition types to highlight quality assurance or diagnostic protocols needing improvement.  

# B. Define 3–5 questions that your analysis will answer.
Financial Variance Question:

1.What is the average billing amount per medical condition, and does significant billing variance exist across different insurance providers and hospitals for the same diagnosis?

Operational Efficiency Question:

2.How does the average Length of Stay (LOS) differ across admission types (Emergency, Elective, Urgent) and medical conditions, and which segments contribute most to bed utilization?

Demographic & Clinical Load Question:

3.Which age brackets and genders represent the highest volume of high-cost admissions and chronic medical conditions (e.g., Cancer, Diabetes, Obesity)?

Quality of Care / Diagnostic Accuracy Question:

4.What proportion of diagnostic test results are returned as "Inconclusive" or "Abnormal," and are these outcomes concentrated within specific hospitals or medical conditions?

Physician Performance / Load Balancing Question:

5.Is patient billing or length of stay skewed towards specific doctors or hospitals, indicating operational bottlenecks or uneven workload distribution?

# C. Define at least 2 hypotheses that you want to test.
Hypothesis 1 (Financial / Payer Dynamics):
Null Hypothesis (H.): There is no significant difference in the average Billing Amount charged for the same Medical Condition across different Insurance Provider groups (e.g., Medicare, Blue Cross, Aetna).
Alternative Hypothesis (H1): Certain insurance providers incur significantly higher average billing amounts for identical medical conditions, indicating inconsistent rate agreements or differential billing practices.

Hypothesis 2 (Operational / Admission Type Impact):
Null Hypothesis (H.): Emergency admissions do not result in a significantly longer Length of Stay (LOS) compared to Elective or Urgent admissions.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1): Emergency admissions have a significantly higher mean Length of Stay (LOS) than Elective admissions due to higher patient severity and delayed diagnostic processing.
