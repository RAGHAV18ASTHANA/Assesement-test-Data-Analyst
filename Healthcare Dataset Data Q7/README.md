Actionable Management RecommendationsFix Laboratory Test Protocols (High Impact | High Feasibility)Action: Calibrate lab machinery and set up clear re-testing protocols.   
Backed by: Insight 1 (33.1% of diagnostic tests returned as "Inconclusive").   
Who acts: Chief Medical Officer & Lab Director.   
Outcome: Eliminates secondary re-testing costs and gets patients diagnosed faster.   
KPI: Inconclusive test percentage (Target: drop from 33.1% to under 10%).   

Move to Acuity-Based Billing (High Impact | Moderate Feasibility)Action: Transition from flat-band pricing to tiered billing that reflects how resource-intensive a condition actually is.   
Backed by: Insight 2 (Flat billing rates across complex vs. chronic conditions).   
Who acts: CFO & Revenue Cycle Team.   
Outcome: Stops margin loss on high-severity cases like oncology.   
KPI: Billed margin variance across high vs. low acuity cases.   

Fast-Track Elective Discharges (Moderate Impact | High Feasibility)Action: Set up dedicated discharge planning for elective patients to free up beds.   
Backed by: Insight 3 (Emergency stays average 15.58 days, virtually equal to scheduled elective stays at 15.51 days).   
Who acts: COO & Nursing Directors.   
Outcome: Faster bed turnover and fewer bottlenecks in emergency intake.   
KPI: Average Length of Stay for Elective Admissions (Target: reduce from 15.5 days to under 8 days).
